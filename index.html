About Me

Hi, I'm Pavle

I'm Mathematical Grammar School student from Belgrade, Serbia, with a passion for science, technology, and outdoor activities.

I hope to pursue a career in science, so my studies cover a broad range of topics across physics, mathematics, and IT. I enjoy participating in competitions, constantly challenging myself to learn and grow - so far, I have won silver medals representing Serbia on three international science olympiads: International Junior Science Olympiad 2023, Balkan Physics Olympiad 2025 and International Olympiad on Astronomy and Astrophysics 2025.

I'm the Head of the Website Department at IJSO Guide, an organization aiming to level the playing field and help students worldwide excel in science competitions by providing free, high-quality resources crafted by former olympiad medalists.

When I'm not studying or coding, you can find me cycling through beautiful landscapes or capturing moments with my camera.
